# Traditions and Vocabulary of Mars College

Mars College has developed its own calendar of traditions and its own vernacular
over seven seasons. This glossary explains the terms an outsider will encounter.

## The season's rituals

- **Build Week / the build:** the collective construction of the campus each
  November–December — the founding act of every season.
- **Ideas Week:** the season-opening week (mid-January) when members pitch projects
  and the year's work takes shape.
- **Thunder Talks:** the signature weekly lightning-talk series — short member
  presentations at the Chiba amphitheater, running every season since 2022.
- **Mars Electronica:** the end-of-season exhibition festival (late March) — a
  gallery show, final presentations, the AI film festival, and the Mars Awards.
- **Moon raves:** desert parties on full and new moons, often with live-coded
  electronic music.
- **Holi:** the spring color festival, celebrated on the playa.
- **Mahashivaratri:** an all-night festival that in recent years culminates in
  burning a large geometric temple structure built during the season.
- **The unbuild:** the end-of-season teardown — striking structures, inventorying
  materials, and returning the desert to bare ground.

## Places

- **Bombay Beach:** the small town on the Salton Sea that anchors the community;
  also home of the Bombay Beach Biennale art festival, which Mars participates in.
- **The playa / Mars campus:** the off-grid desert parcel where the seasonal city is
  built.
- **Chiba:** the central pallet-rack hall — kitchen, bar, library, media cave, and
  amphitheater — rebuilt each season and named for the city in William Gibson's
  *Neuromancer*.
- **Chatsubo:** the bar inside Chiba, also a *Neuromancer* reference; the social
  center of camp.
- **Saturn:** the community house in Bombay Beach that serves as the social and
  AI anchor of the off-season.
- **Venus:** a community compound — workshop, library, and showers.
- **Freeside:** the residential village-within-the-village, with its own café and
  co-op rhythm — the ground the Freeside camp (below) is built on.
- **Mimos:** the community café — coffee and coworking.
- **Slab City:** the long-established off-grid community nearby, on the site of a
  decommissioned military base; Martians and Slab City residents visit back and
  forth.

## People words

- **Martian:** a member of the Mars College community. "We are all Martians."
- **Camp:** a group of people committing to live together as a cluster for the
  season — the basic social unit of the village.
- **DPW:** the Department of Public Works — the build organization that raises,
  maintains, and strikes the village. https://dpw.mars.college
- **DOM:** the Department of Makers — Mars's makerspace and
  workshop camp, known as **Tool Camp** through the 2026 season and renamed for
  2027. https://dom.mars.college
- **MIT:** the Mars Institute of Technology — the AI school, grown out of what
  earlier seasons called AI Camp or the Creative AI major.
  https://mars.college/mit
- **Co Lab:** the Community Lab camp — a three-month practice in coherent
  community, sociocratic governance, and daily ritual.
  https://colab.mars.college
- **MMA:** the Martian Music Academy — twelve weeks of making songs and building
  a scene, performing on a stepped pyramid of pallet racks raised for the season.
  https://music.mars.college
- **Freeside:** the culture camp, and Mars's original on-campus living camp — a
  cooperative of eight to twelve Martians sharing a kitchen, a meal plan, chores,
  and a lounge, tied to no single discipline. https://freeside.mars.college
- **Treasury:** the transparent community fund that pays for shared services.
- **Council / circles:** the elected and volunteer bodies through which the
  community governs itself.
- **Do-ocracy:** the operating principle that decisions are made by the people doing
  the work.
