# Visit Mars College

> When to visit Mars College: two public showcase weekends in 2027, Midterms (Feb 12–14) and Mars Electronica (Mar 26–28), held at the camp near Bombay Beach, California. Page for humans: https://mars.college/visit

## Where and when

Mars College is an annual pop-up, off-grid college town on a dry lake bed near Bombay Beach, on the Salton Sea, in the California desert. The 2027 season (Season 8) runs from January 11 to late March. The community raises the village from pallet racks and plywood each winter and takes it down again in spring.

## The two public weekends

| Dates (2027) | Weekend | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feb 12–14 | Midterms | Mid-season. The campus pauses and everyone presents where they are in their Mars journey. |
| Mar 26–28 | Mars Electronica | End-of-season exhibition and finals: an art exhibition, final presentations, and the Mars AI Film Festival screened outdoors. Open to locals and Martians alike. |

Note: other pages (the 2027 timeline, the MIT page) call the midterm "Declaration Days" and give Feb 13–15. The Feb 12–14 dates above are the ones for the public visit page; confirm exact dates and times with the team before travelling.
